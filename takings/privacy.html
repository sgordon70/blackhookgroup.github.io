Takings Privacy

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version

Takings collects nothing. There is no Takings server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your App Store Connect API key (Issuer ID, Key ID, vendor number and private key) is stored in your Mac's Keychain. The sales reports, subscriber counts and reviews Takings downloads are stored in the app's own folder on your Mac. Removing the key in Settings deletes them.

Who Takings talks to

Apple, at api.appstoreconnect.apple.com, using your own key, to read your apps, sales reports and reviews. The European Central Bank, to download its public daily exchange-rate file. Nothing about you or your sales is sent to either beyond what Apple's API needs to answer your request.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. Blackhook Group never sees your payment details.
