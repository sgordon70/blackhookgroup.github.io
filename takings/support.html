Takings Support

Yesterday's App Store proceeds in your Mac menu bar.

Why Takings

If you sell apps, you sign in to App Store Connect far too often just to learn whether yesterday made money, and you hear about bad reviews days late. Takings watches for Apple's daily report, puts the number in your menu bar, and tells you when a 1 or 2 star review lands.

Getting started

Takings uses your own App Store Connect API key. On first launch it walks you through six steps: make a key with Admin access, download the .p8 file, then enter the Key ID, Issuer ID and vendor number. Test connection checks each part before you save.

Why is yesterday $0?

Takings counts sales and first-time downloads. Updates, re-downloads and restored purchases are left out. Report days are Pacific Time, so "yesterday" follows Apple's calendar, not your clock.

Why does this differ from Payments?

Takings estimates proceeds from Apple's daily Sales report and converts other currencies with European Central Bank reference rates. Your Payments page is final and is paid by Apple's fiscal month.

I am not getting notifications

Notifications are part of the one-time unlock. Takings checks only while it is running, so turn on Launch at Login in Settings. Also check System Settings, Notifications, Takings.

Does it cost anything?

Free: connect your key, yesterday's numbers in the menu bar, and your reviews. A one-time unlock adds the last 30 days, each app on its own, and the report and bad review alerts. No subscription. To restore, open Overview and click Restore purchase.

Where is my data?

Only on your devices. Your key is in your Keychain (synced by iCloud Keychain to your own Mac and iPhone, if you use it) and your reports are in the app's own folder. Takings has no server. Removing the key in Settings deletes downloaded reports.
