Privacy Policy

The short version: Mahjong Play doesn't collect anything. Your games, your cards and your scores stay on your device.

Last updated October 3, 2026

In short

  • Mahjong Play has no backend server. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • Your saved games, statistics, settings and the cards you build are stored only on your device.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • The computer opponents run entirely on your device.

01 What we collect

Nothing. Mahjong Play has no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We, the developer, never see your hands, your cards, your scores or anything else you do in the app.

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That is handled by Apple, not by the app.

02 What stays on your device Never synced

Mahjong Play remembers your saved games so you can continue later, your solitaire statistics and best times, your settings, and any cards you build for American Mah Jongg. This is stored using Apple's standard on-device app storage and never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The card lines you enter are your own. Mahjong Play does not include or transmit any official card.

03 Who we share data with

Nobody. Mahjong Play doesn't use any third-party services, advertising networks or analytics platforms.

04 Children's privacy

Mahjong Play collects no data from anyone, so this applies equally regardless of age.

05 Changes to this policy

If Mahjong Play's data practices ever change in a future update (for example, if online play with friends is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change.
