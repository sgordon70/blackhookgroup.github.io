Privacy Policy

The short version: BurgerStack doesn't collect anything. Everything you do stays on your iPhone.

Last updated September 29, 2026

In short

  • BurgerStack has no backend server. Nothing you do in the app is ever sent anywhere.
  • Your best Order Rush scores are saved locally on your device and never uploaded.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • BurgerStack is a one-time paid app with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
What we collect What stays on your device Who we share data with Children's privacy Changes to this policy Contact

01 What we collect

Nothing. BurgerStack has no account system, no network calls, and no analytics SDKs of any kind. We, the developer, never see the burgers you build, your scores, or anything else you do in the app.

The only information involved anywhere is the standard purchase record Apple itself keeps for any paid App Store app, used solely to confirm you bought BurgerStack. We don't have access to that beyond what Apple shows every developer in App Store Connect.

02 What stays on your device Never synced

BurgerStack remembers your best Order Rush score for each difficulty, and your best Daily Special score for each day, so you have something to beat. This is stored using Apple's standard on-device app storage (UserDefaults) and never leaves your phone.

Share cards are created on your iPhone. They only leave it if you choose to share or save one yourself, through the standard iOS share sheet.

03 Who we share data with

Nobody. BurgerStack doesn't use any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. The only party involved in anything is Apple, through the App Store's standard purchase processing, the same as any paid app.

04 Children's privacy

BurgerStack is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, so this applies equally regardless of age.

05 Changes to this policy

If BurgerStack's data practices ever change in a future update, this page will be updated to reflect that, and the "last updated" date above will change.
