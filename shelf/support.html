Shelf Support

A list of the things in your house that run out.

How it works

Add an item, a photo of the box, and a date. Shelf reminds you at 9:00 that morning. When you buy more, swipe the item right and tap Bought. The item stays on your list without a date until you set a new one. Swipe left to snooze it 3 or 7 days.

Does it cost anything?

Your first 5 items are free and do everything. A one-time $4.99 unlock lets the list keep growing. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Restore my unlock

Tap + when you have 5 items, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I am not getting reminders

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then Shelf, and turn on Allow Notifications. Shelf only reminds you about items that have a date.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Shelf has no server and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your list.
