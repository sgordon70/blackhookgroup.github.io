Privacy Policy

Shelf, by Blackhook Group LLC. Effective October 4, 2026.

Shelf collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Item names, notes, photos, and dates you add.
  • Reminder schedules, which are local notifications created by your iPhone.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. There is no server, no account, and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes the list.

Camera and photos

Shelf uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo of an item. The photo is stored inside the app on your device.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No crash reporting services. No selling or sharing data.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Children

Shelf is not directed at children under 13.
