Joining a trip

Get the app, tap Join with a code, and type the 8-character code your maid of honor sent. Then add your name. No account needed.

Maid of honor on a new phone

When you planned the trip you got a maid of honor code (like ABCD-EFGH-JKMN). On the new phone tap Maid of honor on a new phone? and enter the trip code and that code. If you lost it, open the trip on your old phone and look under Your maid of honor code, or email us from the address you'd like us to reply to.

The trip pass

The maid of honor buys it once per trip ($14.99). It unlocks the photo wall and the recap poster for everyone in that trip. Planning, money, packing, the guide and SOS are always free. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Money

BachPlan works out who owes whom with the fewest payments. Pay buttons open Venmo, Cash App or PayPal with the amount filled in; BachPlan never touches the money.

Emergencies

If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911. The SOS button in the app lists Nashville emergency rooms and urgent care.
