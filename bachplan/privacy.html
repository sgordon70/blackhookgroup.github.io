BachPlan

Privacy Policy

BachPlan, by Blackhook Group LLC. Effective October 9, 2026.

BachPlan helps a group plan a Nashville bachelorette weekend. There are no accounts: no email, no password, no phone number required. Here is exactly what the app keeps and why.

What we store

  • A random device sign-in. When you plan or join a trip, your phone gets an anonymous sign-in so the app knows which trips are yours. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or Apple or Google account.
  • Trip details the maid of honor enters: the bride's first name, her own name, the dates, and the poster theme.
  • Your name and RSVP when you join a trip.
  • The plan: stops, times and notes the maid of honor adds, and which stops you check off.
  • Money: expenses the group logs (what, how much, who paid, how it's split), settle-ups you record, and any payment handles you choose to add (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle phone/email) so others can pay you back.
  • Packing: items anyone adds and your own checkmarks.
  • Photos people add to the trip's photo wall (with the trip pass).
  • A maid of honor recovery code, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so she can move the trip to a new phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, email, or phone number, and we don't use advertising IDs.

Who can see it

  • Everything about a trip is visible only to people in that trip. Joining needs the trip code the maid of honor shares.
  • Photos are private to the trip and are shown through short-lived links.
  • Payment handles you add are visible to people in your trip so they can pay you.

Payments

The trip pass is bought through Apple or Google. We never see card details. We receive a receipt from the store to confirm the purchase and unlock the trip.

BachPlan never moves money between people. Settle-up buttons open Venmo, Cash App or PayPal, which follow their own privacy policies.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing your data. No data used to train AI.

Where it lives

Trip data and photos are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the United States.

Deleting your data

The maid of honor or the person who added it can remove stops, expenses, packing items and photos in the app. To delete a whole trip or everything about you, email hello@blackhookgroup.com with the trip code and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

BachPlan is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
