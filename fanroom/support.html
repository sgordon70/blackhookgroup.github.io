Getting started

How does Fanroom work?

Open the app and tap any game that's live right now. Pick a team to root for if you like, and Fanroom drops you into a small chat with 2–4 other fans watching the same game. The chat stays open until the game ends.

Why can't I join a game?

Chats only open while a game is live. Games starting within the next two hours are listed under "Starting soon" and open the moment they kick off.

Nobody else is in my chat.

After a short wait Fanroom shows how many fans are online for that game and offers a league-wide room (like "All NFL"). If your group goes quiet, a "Find a new group" button moves you into another group for the same game.

Can I change my nickname?

Once. Tap your nickname at the top of the games list. Nicknames have to be clean.

Where did my chat go?

Chats close when the game ends, and messages are deleted 24 hours later. To get back to a chat that's still open, use the "Back to your huddle" button at the top of the games list.

Safety

How do I report a message?

Long-press the message (or tap the ••• next to it) and choose Report message. When three different fans report a message it's hidden, and the sender is muted in that chat.

How do I block someone?

Long-press one of their messages and choose Block, or tap the people button at the top of the chat and tap Block next to their name. You'll never be put in a group with them again.

What gets filtered automatically?

Links are removed, messages with slurs are blocked, and other swearing is starred out.

Billing

Does Fanroom cost anything?

No. Fanroom is free, with no ads.

Privacy

What does Fanroom collect?

An anonymous ID, your nickname and team pick, and the messages you send (deleted 24 hours after the game). No accounts, no analytics and no ads. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Email hello@blackhookgroup.com with your nickname and roughly when you used Fanroom.
