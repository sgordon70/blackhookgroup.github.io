Privacy Policy

IfEnough, by Blackhook Group LLC. Effective October 4, 2026.

IfEnough exists so you can say yes to a plan without anyone knowing, until enough people are in. Privacy is the product, so we keep almost nothing.

What we store

  • Plans: the title, place (and map coordinates if you picked a place from search), time, time zone, and how many people it needs.
  • Your answer: yes or no, and the first name you type when you say yes.
  • A random device code: your phone makes a random code so the server can remember your answer. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email, or Apple or Google account.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you "It's on."

We do not ask for or store your email, phone number, contacts, location, or photos. There are no accounts.

Who sees what

  • While a plan is waiting, nobody, including the person who made it, can see who said yes. The server only ever reports a count.
  • When enough people say yes, the first names of everyone who said yes are shown to people with the plan's link.
  • If a plan never hits its number or is canceled, every answer for it is deleted. Nobody ever learns who said yes.

How long we keep it

Answers on plans that expire or are canceled are deleted right away. Everything else about a plan is deleted 7 days after the plan's time (or 7 days after it ends).

What we never do

No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing data. No data used to train AI.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Where it lives

Plans are stored on Cloudflare servers that run IfEnough. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. If you add a plan to your calendar, that copy is in your calendar app and follows its own rules.

Children

IfEnough is not directed at children under 13.
