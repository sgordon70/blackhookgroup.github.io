IfEnough Support

Plans that happen only if enough people say yes.

How it works

Make a plan, pick how many people it needs, and share the link. Friends answer yes or no, in the app or right in their browser. Nobody sees who said yes until the number is hit. Then it's on, and everyone who said yes sees the list.

Does it cost anything?

Answering is always free. Organizers can have 3 active plans at a time for free. A one-time $4.99 unlock removes the limit. No subscription, no ads.

Restore my unlock

When the unlock screen appears, tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

What happens if it doesn't hit the number?

At the plan's start time it expires. Every answer is deleted. Nobody ever learns who said yes.
